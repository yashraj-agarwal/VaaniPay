Problem Statement 
PS 3: FinTech for Financial Inclusion: While digital payment systems have grown rapidly, a 
large section of the population — including gig workers, small business owners, and rural 
communities — still lacks access to credit, financial planning tools, and protection against 
fraud. The challenge is to develop a solution that leverages technology to make financial 
services more accessible, secure, and inclusive, addressing the needs of underserved 
populations. 
Ideation 
More than 300M Indians have no access to digital financial services, not for want of need, but 
for lack of any solution that doesn’t presuppose use of a smartphone, internet, and literacy in 
English. For the hundreds of millions of Indians who use only a basic feature phone or 
landline to communicate-gig workers, daily laborers, small farmers, rural women-whose 
financial life is conducted entirely in cash, with zero security in terms of savings, constant 
exposure to moneylenders, and permanent exclusion from credit, there is not one mainstream 
financial application. UPI is already the future of payments in urban India, but the 250M 
Indians who will never be able to afford, or will lack reason to use, a smartphone, have no 
access to a single app. 
VaaniPay is founded on one simple insight: do not ask the underserved Indians to update the 
infrastructure they use, bring financial services to the infrastructure they already have. A 
telephone. Any telephone. Using IVR technology, integrated with purpose-built Indian 
language speech models from Sarvam AI, and built on the NPCI's already extant USSD-UPI 
rails, VaaniPay allows anyone with a telephone to dial one number, speak naturally in their 
own language, and access payments, credit, insurance and savings entirely through voice-no 
smartphone, no internet, no literacy required. 
The NPCI's *99# USSD service has already demonstrated the regulatory and technical 
feasibility of using a universal code (a phone number) for banking, however its current, keypad driven interface only supports nine languages, and it includes no support for savings, 
insurance or credit.

Implementation 
When the user dials the VaaniPay number, the call is received by the IVR engine that is 
powered by Twilio or Asterisk. There is no data plan needed on the caller’s phone-the call 
proceeds over the normal PSTN or GSM line. The system automatically detects the user’s 
language from the first spoken phrase by the user, or gives a short language choice if none are 
detected. The entire audio is streamed to Sarvam AI’s STT pipeline, which performs 
transcription with a word error rate under 12% on the lossy, 8KHz telephony audio. 
The transcribed text is then fed to a custom NLP intent parser which is fine-tuned for 
vocabulary around financial domains across Indic languages. This determines one of the 
many intents (payment, balance inquiry, loan application, insurance purchase, credit score 
check, savings deposit etc.) as well as the entities for that intent (amount, recipient, account 
etc.) for the request. Authentication: Before the banking orchestrator attempts to process the 
transaction request downstream, it uses the registered voice print along with a spoken 4-digit 
mPIN in order to verify the identity of the caller. 
The banking orchestrator routes the validated request to the corresponding downstream 
systems (NPCI USSD bridge for UPI payments, NBFC partner APIs for loans, InsurTech 
partner integrations for insurance). After the transaction is confirmed, the response is then 
converted into natural speech using Sarvam AI's TTS engine for the specific user's language, 
and played back over the call. The entire experience from dial to confirmation for a UPI 
payment takes under 50 seconds.

Key Features 
VaaniPay handles all UPI transactions via voice - user speaks the beneficiary's name or 
number and the amount and transaction is executed without any need of any button press or 
keypad. User can inquire about balance, mini statements and transfer money via voice 
commands only. The platform supports fully voice-driven applications for micro loans 
between 500 to 25,000 (with instant decisions taken based on the user's in-app behavioral 
credit score). It also supports fully voice-activated daily health insurance, accident insurance, 
crop insurance, recurring deposit, fixed deposit and allows users to deposit funds via voice. 
The user can also get their EPF/NPS balance from EPFO/PFRDA via respective API 
integration. 
Most unique part is the in-app behavioral credit scoring mechanism to serve the 'New to 
Credit' (NTC) customers who have never had a formal credit history. Score is derived based 
on five categories of signals - consistency of transactions, timely repayment of previous 
micro loans, savings consistency, level of platform usage and strength of referral network. 
Score is calculated on the standard 300-900 credit scoring scale (used by Indian credit 
bureaus). And interestingly, when a user inquires about their credit score, VaaniPay reads out 
the most significant factors contributing to the score back to the user, making the user aware 
of how to improve their credit score as well as grant credit access to them. Loans are 
sanctioned and interest rate is regulated and depends on credit score (for score >650 they can 
avail higher loan amount and lower interest rates).

Tech Stack 
Speech intelligence layer: The STT and TTS for all 22 Indian languages were completely 
built in-house at Sarvam AI specifically for Indic speech and not an adapted version of 
models that have been fine tuned on English data. As a result they perform significantly 
better in telephony settings. IVR and telephony layer: The solution uses either Twilio or 
Asterisk (depending on the deployment requirements of cloud vs. On-prem) for PSTN and 
GSM call routing where the internet is not required for the caller's phone. 
Backend layer: Built on Node.js and Python micro services. NLP intent parser: Custom 
model fine tuned on Indic languages with a finance lexicon. Data storage: User profiles, 
transaction data, behavioral credit data are stored in PostgreSQL. User session is stored in 
Redis to maintain state through multi-turn voice interactions within a call. Payment 
execution: Payments are executed through the NPCI *99# USSD bridge. Loan disbursal is 
achieved via NBFC partner APIs. Insurance offerings can be integrated with InsurTech 
partners licensed by IRDAI like Digit, Acko.

Feasibility 
The technology stack has been validated, with Sarvam AI's models performing at production-
level accuracy under telephony circumstances and NPCI's *99# system processingmillions of 
USSD transactions per day. The regulatory framework is also in place-USSD based UPI is 
already being implemented under RBI regulations from 2014, voice-based KYC is in place 
from 2020 (V-CIP by RBI), micro-lending to NBFC partners is under current frameworks, 
and micro-insurance with IRDAI licensed partners is already a model (e.g. PMJBY/PMSBY). 
Commercially, VaaniPay is viable, without any need for grants and subsidies. Revenue is 
generated via transaction fees of 0.5%-1% on the payments, and loan origination fees on 
loans distributed via NBFC partners. B2B licensing of the voice banking API to NBFCs, 
banks and NGOs to act as a white-labeled voice banking platform will provide enterprise 
revenue to the business in addition to consumer revenue streams. The existing partnership 
with the government via Jan Dhan Yojana and PM Fasal Bima provides a ready distribution 
pipeline into the exact segment for whom VaaniPay was built.

Impact 
VaaniPay's immediate outcome is access – 250 million feature phone owners given first 
formal digital finance journey. The second and more significant outcome is structural. Each 
transaction creates a behavioral credit profile, that in 90 days, for a day-wage earner with no 
existing credit score, qualifies them for a micro-loan at a regulation rate-saving them from 
24-60% annually from lenders-and for each activated micro-insurance, a farmer or domestic 
worker has protection against the health and accident shocks that could erase 6 months of 
saving. 
 Women in rural communities are the primary users, excluded by default in smartphone 
dominated digital finance. A zero-literacy, zero-smartphone, native-language interface drops 
every wall that's excluded them traditionally from finance. In scale, VaaniPay doesn't merely 
serve unbanked India; it formally includes them-Users with a credit history from VaaniPay 
are ready for mainstream banks products, the platform acts as a floor, not a ceiling. 
 
There are 1.15 Billion phone subscribers in India. Every one of those is in VaaniPay's 
addressable market. The architecture scales seamlessly to Bangladesh, Nepal, and Sri Lanka 
upon expansion of Sarvam Language Models, and is replicable in any emerging market that 
exhibits feature phone dominance. The problem VaaniPay is addressing is not India's unique 
problem, it is the developing world's dominant financial inclusion problem.
